## Goal

By the end of the lab you will be able to:
- load and prepare the **MNIST** dataset,
- understand the **reshape → normalize → one-hot** steps,
- build, compile and train a **`Sequential`** neural network,
- and interpret the training outcome (`loss` / `accuracy`).
